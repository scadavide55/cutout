Questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Each image is decoded, cut out and exported inside this tab, on your own GPU or CPU. Your browser only downloads the page, the AI runtime and the engine you pick, never your pictures. The privacy policy lists every host involved.

Which engine should I pick?

Low is the quickest and works well for portraits and simple backgrounds. Medium suits people, products and pets. High handles hair, fur and tricky edges on a capable GPU, and Ultra refines High with a second pass on high-end devices. Engines your device can't run safely are dimmed, with the reason shown. The model guide goes deeper.

Why is the first cut-out slower?

The engine downloads the first time you use it, between 6.6 MB and 223 MB depending on which one you pick. Your browser keeps it in its cache and reuses it for the whole batch, so later runs are much faster.

What can I export?

A transparent PNG, a JPG on a colour or on a photo of your own, a copy to the clipboard, or every result at once as a ZIP. Exports keep the original resolution.

Are there any limits?

Up to 20 images per batch, 30 MB each. A single image can be up to 24 megapixels on a computer and 12 on a phone, with at most 8192 px per side.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no watermark, no ads. The code is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence.
