## **Model guide**
 _This model guide is made so you can pick the right engine for the job. As of 14 Oct 2026, Cutout offers 2 engines: Fast and Pro._
 _They are being renamed: Fast becomes Low, Pro becomes High._
 _Two more are on the way: Medium and Ultra._

**Low (currently "Fast")**
An entry-level model. Use it on very limited devices, or when you need the fastest possible speed.
Pros: Very fast, runs on practically anything, strong on portraits and well-focused subjects.
Cons: Dated. Struggles with fur (dogs, cats and animals generally), complex shapes such as trees, cramped framing, and it leaves traces of hair and stray edges.
Uses the MODNet model.

**Medium**
A compressed version of the High model. Keeps roughly 75% of its capability at a much smaller size.
Pros: Clearly better than Low and much better on animals and objects. Nearly High-level results at a fraction of the cost.
Cons: Still a large download, and it is slower on limited devices. Less tested than Low and High.
Uses a compressed version of the BEN2 model.
_Warning: it is in beta and may produce unexpected results._

**High (currently "Pro")**
The full-weights version of Medium. A big model, built for mid-to-high-end phones or laptops.
Pros: Very powerful. Near top-tier results while still runnable on phones. Widely tested and stable. Good on almost everything.
Cons: Heavy. Only runs on higher-end phones, and it still is not Ultra-grade.
Uses the full BEN2 model.

**Ultra**
A genuinely heavy model that only runs comfortably on high-end phones and good laptops.
Pros: The best in the line, good on everything.
Cons: Really heavy.
Not available yet.
